Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

Summary: Entropy Studio performs its calculations locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal information or sensitive wallet material. The App does not require an account and does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the Entropy Studio mobile application (the "App", "we", "us", or "our"). Entropy Studio is a local tool for working with entropy, BIP39 recovery phrases and passphrases, private-key material, and Bitcoin wallet derivation data.

Information Processed on Your Device

Depending on the feature you use, the App may process dice rolls, card draws, number-base input, BIP39 recovery phrases and passphrases, private-key input, and derived wallet information such as extended keys, descriptors, and addresses. This information can be highly sensitive.

These calculations are performed locally on your device. The App does not send the information you enter or derive to us or to third-party services.

Information We Do Not Collect

Entropy Studio does not collect personal information or sensitive wallet material. In particular, we do not collect:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account credentials
  • Recovery phrases, passphrases, private keys, wallet descriptors, or derived wallet data
  • Device identifiers, advertising identifiers, or location data
  • Usage, diagnostic, or crash-reporting data
  • Analytics, advertising, or tracking data

Storage and Retention

Local, In-Memory Processing

The App holds current inputs and results in app memory while you use it. The App does not intentionally save this information to persistent application storage, synchronize it between devices, or back it up to a service we operate.

No Accounts or Cloud Service

Entropy Studio does not offer user accounts, registration, login, cloud synchronization, or developer-operated storage for your information. Because we do not receive your information, we cannot recover it for you.

Data Sharing and Tracking

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share personal information or wallet material. The App does not include third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash-reporting services.

Security

Recovery phrases, passphrases, and private keys can grant access to digital assets. Keep them private, use the App in a secure environment, and do not share them with anyone you do not trust. Local processing reduces data transmission, but it cannot protect information if your device or its display is compromised.

Children's Privacy

The App does not collect personal information from any user, including children under 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App or our privacy practices change. We will post the updated policy here and revise the "Last updated" date above.
