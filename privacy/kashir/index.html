Kashir Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Kashir is a self-custodial Cashu wallet. You can use it without creating a Kashir account. Your device stores your recovery phrase and wallet records. When you use a mint or the optional ad rewards feature, Kashir sends the information those services need to complete your request.

Wallet data on your device

Kashir stores your recovery phrase in your device’s secure credential storage. The wallet database on your device stores Cashu proofs, transaction records, and payment quotes. Kashir also saves your mint preferences and keeps records of reward redemptions so it can retry an interrupted payment.

Kashir does not send your recovery phrase to a mint, the ad site, or a Kashir server.

Cashu mints and payments

Kashir connects to the mints you configure, including mints associated with Cashu tokens you receive. A mint may receive Lightning invoices, amounts, payment quotes, and Cashu protocol data, including proofs submitted when you spend or exchange them. Mints can see network information such as your IP address and handle that information under their own privacy practices.

Watch Ads

Watch Ads opens a third-party rewards site. When you claim a reward, Kashir uses the LNURL withdrawal link to request payment. Kashir sends the withdrawal identifier and a Lightning invoice to the rewards service, then redeems the payment through your active mint.

The rewards site and its ad providers may use cookies or similar technologies and receive information such as your IP address, browser details, and ad interactions. Their own privacy practices apply. You can avoid this feature by leaving Watch Ads unused.

Analytics and tracking

Kashir does not include its own analytics or cross-site tracking tools. Third-party mints and the rewards site handle the information they receive according to their own privacy practices.

Camera and clipboard

Kashir uses the camera when you open its QR scanner to read payment data. Scanning takes place on your device. You can revoke camera access in your device settings. When you choose to copy wallet information, Kashir places it on your device’s clipboard.

Retention and deletion

Kashir keeps wallet data locally until you remove it. In Kashir’s settings, removing the stored recovery phrase resets local wallet data, mint settings, and redemption history. Back up your recovery phrase and withdraw any funds you need before resetting the wallet. Mints and the rewards site manage their own records, and records retained by those services may remain subject to their policies.

Support and policy updates

If you email us, we use the information you provide to respond to your request. For privacy questions, contact support@kashir.xyz. Never send your recovery phrase or unspent Cashu tokens to support.

We will post policy changes here and update the date at the top.
